Sakumono, between Accra and Tema

Sakumono sits in the Tema Metropolitan District, built up around Sakumono Lagoon — a coastal wetland that's kept the area known for its beach as much as its estates. Google's own area score rates the neighbourhood 2.8 for visitors, citing it as good for sightseeing, recreation, and getting around.
