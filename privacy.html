Privacy notice

How information moves when you use this website to ask about a stay.

Information you provide

The enquiry form asks for your name, email address, phone number, check-in and check-out dates, and guest count. Room preference and message are optional. Please do not include payment details or other sensitive information.

How enquiries are handled

When you submit the form, your details are sent to FormSubmit so it can deliver the enquiry to the property email address. FormSubmit says it keeps submissions in its archive for 30 days; copies delivered to the property email may remain there according to the email provider's retention and the property's handling. See FormSubmit's documentation.

The website does not create user accounts or store enquiries in its own database. An enquiry is used to respond about accommodation and is not an instant booking confirmation.

WhatsApp and other external services

The WhatsApp button can place the details you entered into a pre-filled message. The message is not sent until you choose to send it in WhatsApp. Google Maps is embedded on the contact page, and Google Fonts are loaded for the site typography. Links to WhatsApp, Google Maps, Instagram, and Facebook take you to services with their own privacy practices. Those providers may receive technical information from your browser when their content or links are used.

Questions or data requests

For questions about an enquiry received by the property, contact dominionluxuryapartment@gmail.com. Requests about information held by FormSubmit should be directed to that provider.
